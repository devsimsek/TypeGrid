# About Lens & Light

Welcome to my portfolio! I am a photographer specializing in **urban geometry** and *natural landscapes*. My work explores the intersection of light, shadow, and composition in both natural and built environments.

## My Approach

I believe in keeping things simple and letting the environment speak for itself. Whether it's the chaotic neon streets of a metropolis or the quiet serenity of an alpine ridge, I strive to capture the raw essence of the moment.

### Gear I Use

I shoot primarily with a mix of digital and film cameras:
- Canon 5D Mark IV
- Fujifilm X-T4
- Sony A7R V
- Canon AE-1 (35mm Film)
